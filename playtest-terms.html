Playtest Participation Terms

Last updated: May 24, 2026

These terms describe what to expect if you apply for and participate in the insignificANTS closed playtest. By submitting an application, you confirm you have read and agreed to them. The playtest is operated by Someone Else's Shoes Interactive LLC, a Michigan-based independent game studio.

01 Eligibility

To apply for the playtest, you must be at least 18 years of age. We may further restrict participation based on platform availability, language support, geographic region, or other practical considerations. We test on iOS and Android devices, and you'll need access to one of these to participate meaningfully. The playtest is not yet open. Sign up now to be considered when the first round begins.

02 Discretion to admit and remove

Participation in the playtest is by application and at our sole discretion. We reserve the right to:

  • Accept or decline any applicant, without explanation
  • Remove any participant from the playtest at any time, for any reason
  • Limit the size of the closed playtest
  • End the closed playtest when we determine the game is ready for public release

03 What you receive

If accepted, you will receive:

  • Access to pre-release builds of insignificANTS, delivered through Apple TestFlight, Google Play Internal Testing, or a similar distribution channel
  • A channel to provide feedback, typically through email, an in-app form, or a community space
  • Notifications when new builds become available

Playtest access is provided free of charge. We do not pay playtest participants, and we do not charge for playtest access.

04 Confidentiality

Playtest builds are pre-release software not intended for the general public. As a participant, you agree not to publicly share screenshots, video, gameplay footage, or detailed mechanical descriptions of unreleased content without our written permission. You also agree not to share build files, login credentials, or playtest access with anyone else.

Discussing high-level impressions is fine ("I'm in the insignificANTS playtest and finding it interesting"). Sharing specific spoilers, detailed pre-release content, or recordings of unreleased features is not.

Content creators interested in covering the playtest should reach out separately at hello@sesinteractive.com for permission and arrangements.

05 Feedback and ideas

When you provide feedback, suggestions, bug reports, or ideas during the playtest, you grant SES Interactive a perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable, transferable license to use that feedback to improve the game and our other products, without any obligation to attribute, compensate, or acknowledge you.

In plain English If you suggest something and we use it, we don't owe you anything for it. This is standard for closed playtests and exists so we can act on community feedback without legal risk on either side.

06 Credits

We may, at our sole discretion, include playtest participants in the game's release credits as a thank-you. Inclusion is not guaranteed and is at our discretion. We typically prioritize participants who provide substantial, constructive feedback over those who only install and play.

If included, your name or chosen handle will appear as you provided it during signup. You may request to be removed from credits at any time before the game releases by emailing us.

07 No employment or business relationship

Playtest participation is unpaid and creates no employment, contractor, partnership, joint venture, or business relationship between you and SES Interactive. You participate as a volunteer testing pre-release software because you find it interesting.

08 Personal information

We collect the information you provide during signup, typically including your email address, name or handle, and information about your interest in ants and games. We use this information to operate the closed playtest, communicate about the game's eventual release, and decide who to admit.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. You may request removal from the closed playtest and deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing hello@sesinteractive.com.

09 The game is in development

The playtest builds reflect a work in progress. Features may be incomplete, broken, or absent entirely. Performance may be inconsistent. Save data may not transfer between builds. Content you encounter in the playtest may change significantly or be removed before public release. The final released game will differ from any version you play during the playtest.

We make no warranties about the playtest builds. Use them at your own discretion, and don't rely on them for anything important.

10 Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the closed playtest evolves. Significant changes will be communicated to active participants via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11 Contact

Questions about the closed playtest or these terms can be sent to hello@sesinteractive.com.
